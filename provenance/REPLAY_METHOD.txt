SCIENTIFIC REPLAY AND HISTORICAL PROVENANCE

The public entry point is scripts/run_analysis.py. It rebuilds predictions from
the deposited processed expression matrices, evaluates them, runs all declared
diagnostics, and compares fresh numerical results with immutable earlier values.
No table is copied from reference_tables into the new scientific results.

Historical engine names and protocol identifiers are retained under historical/
so the exact source-code hashes recorded during the study remain verifiable.
These are provenance identifiers, not extra independent validation studies.
Public stage names describe the corresponding scientific analysis.

Fresh primary fitting includes the discovery four-way comparison, discovery
donor-selection diagnostic, separate Arabidopsis three-factor study, and both
citrus genotypes. Fresh model arrays must match the recorded arrays before later
diagnostics execute. Basis comparison permits rotations/signs within the same
orthonormal row space; prediction values, genes, settings and numerical scores
must agree to the stated tolerances.

The span, conditional-noise and citrus gene-attribution diagnostics retain their
historically fixed feature panels and predictor definitions. Their source-level
primary predictions have just been independently reconstructed by the replay.
The diagnostics then reread expression values and calculate their scores anew.
The repeatability stage also performs all 1,024 complementary-pool refits.

All 36,000 conditional simulation outcomes are regenerated with the recorded
seed and batch size. Simulation quantiles are conditional sensitivity summaries,
not calibrated biological P values. All 256 repeatability partitions per
genotype are used; these partitions repeatedly reuse the same original pools.

Functional memberships are frozen external annotation inputs. They are not
reconstructed from the original GAF/GO database archive during this default run.
Their exact hashes, ontology version, original sources and mapping limitations
are in historical/stress_combinations_code9/annotation/. Functional summary
statistics ARE recomputed from fresh gains using those fixed memberships.

Every outcome was already known before release preparation. The fitting versus
target-reading separation checks code behavior and preserves the original
protocol, but release replay cannot turn the targets into new unseen evidence.
The source normalization can couple samples. RNA pools, not genes or partition
draws, are the independent repeated-measurement units assumed in the study.

Numerical validation uses two complementary comparisons:
1. Fresh predictor arrays versus recorded frozen arrays, including exact feature
   identities and projection-space checks in both directions.
2. Fifty-nine compact result tables versus unchanged historical goldens, plus
   two documented canonical noise-diagnostic references introduced in 1.0.1.
   Their original historical references remain pinned and preserved. The
   correction uses the exact single-span identity for CT/S/EL/HS diagnostics,
   retains raw output backups and changes no predictions or simulation draws.
   Numeric tolerance is still atol=rtol=5e-10. Two declared singular-value JSON
   arrays are compared numerically; other labels and missingness remain exact.

Historical independent numerical audits are retained as provenance. The release
validation is an additional reproducibility test, not a replacement for those
independent implementations and not an assertion of native Windows execution.

Source expression files and imported annotations remain third-party research
data. Their presence here does not grant new rights or place them under the
software license. Cite the GEO records and original studies.
