CODE5 numerical and interpretation review

Both audits passed. Main prediction and metric reconstruction used independent
small-Gram eigendecomposition and explicit factorial inclusion-exclusion.
Current-target input exclusion was separately tested by poisoning its three
columns in a temporary source fixture; all predictions and selections were
unchanged. Subspace geometry was reconstructed without production imports;
the disjoint-reference extension used explicit distinct-library products.

WHAT THE SELECTION RESULT SUPPORTS
At primary rank two, common_low1 and raw_low1 select the same donors for every
target; common_low2 and raw_low2 also match for every target. This is a negative
result for any claim that common-axis selection adds value over distance from
the control in this dataset. It does not erase the fixed four-way transfer.

The common_low2 rule beats the all-donor mean in the two quiet triple targets
and the four-way target, but is worse in the two active triple targets. The
control mean is better than common_low2 in both quiet triple targets. Thus
no universal donor-selection advantage follows. Removing the common component
also fails to create a generally superior predictor.

Primary common_low2 memberships are unchanged in every conditional one-pool
omission comparison for all five targets. This stability is not predictive
validity: it occurs even in tasks where the rule performs poorly. Keep the
conditional fixed-basis interpretation and do not call these new replications.

WHAT THE SUBSPACE RESULT SUPPORTS
The previously observed four-way quiet-centroid MSE gain decomposes into
approximately 0.000258 in the top two directions, 0.020023 in the remaining
observed lower-order directions, and 0.021371 outside the full observed
lower-order span. The latter two components each contribute positive realized
gain in all three target libraries. This resolves a real ambiguity in the
rank-two residual: it includes both omitted lower-order directions and an
outside-span component.

The separate disjoint-reference sensitivity also retains positive outside-span
alignment and population proximity margins in all three overlapping control
splits. Those estimates are not numerical corrections to the original gains,
and their different gene filter/reference/basis must remain explicit.

Use 'outside the observed lower-order span'. That span contains sampling noise
and covers the observed condition means only. Outside-span expression is not
automatically a pure higher-order factorial interaction, a new regulatory
program, stress memory, or physiological benefit. Conversely, a high-order
factorial interaction can lie inside the lower-order span.

All these expression values were already examined before CODE5. The subspace
amendment explicitly followed its earlier gain result. No independent biological
validation or newly blinded test was performed. The supported paper direction
is quantitative, resolution-dependent transfer and its limits; a new selection
algorithm or general law is not supported by this stage.
