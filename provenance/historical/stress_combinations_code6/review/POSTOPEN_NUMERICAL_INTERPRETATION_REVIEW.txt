PRIMARY WT RESULT
At the fixed mean-FPKM >=1 threshold, 19,180 genes were retained. Control MSE
is 0.701011984; full pair centroid MSE is 0.117441720.
The mean full-centroid gain is 0.583570264, of which outside-span gain is
0.062273019 and within-span gain is 0.521297245. These sum exactly under
orthogonal decomposition. Outside gain is 10.67% of the total
centroid gain, or 8.88% of control MSE. Most bulk gain
is therefore inside the fitted single-stress span. Adding the fixed outside
component to the within-only predictor reduces its MSE by
34.65% (from 0.179714739 to
0.117441720). These are different denominators and must not
be interchanged. All three target-library outside gains are positive.

The full centroid has lower observed MSE than the fixed single-additive and
second-order-factorial comparators in this experiment. This is a descriptive
comparison among the prespecified models, not proof of global optimality or
a new universally superior algorithm. Three pooled biological RNA libraries
per condition are the replication unit; genes are not biological replicates.
The mutant and threshold results are sensitivity/secondary analyses in the
same experiment; they do not constitute five additional external studies.

WHY THE DISJOINT MARGINS ARE LARGER
The primary 0.062273019 is a realized per-library prediction contribution,
using the CT-mean reference, its three-single mean span, and the 19,180-gene
CT-plus-singles filtered panel. The disjoint-control margins about 0.208–0.242
use 19,217 genes selected from singles only, a different single-CT fitted span
in each fold, two other distinct CT references, symmetrization, target and
donor condition means, and a sampling-covariance correction. They are a
separate population-geometry diagnostic under a different projection and
reference construction. They should corroborate the sign, not be equated to
or substituted for the realized primary prediction gain. Larger disjoint
margins do not demonstrate stronger predictive performance or identify the
magnitude of a 'true' biological interaction. Overlapping folds are not
independent validation datasets.

ALGEBRAIC EXPLANATION, NOT A NEW FITTED MODEL
The prespecified second-order predictor has outside displacement 3r when the
pair centroid has outside displacement r. Using already-opened fixed vectors,
its outside gain is 6*<y,r>-9*||r||^2 = -0.187889827, versus the centroid's
0.062273019. This deterministic post-opening explanatory calculation is
consistent with excess outside amplitude in this comparator here; it does
not estimate an optimal shrinkage rule or establish a regulatory mechanism.
The primary inference remains transferable structure outside a fitted noisy
single-stress span. Neither positive residual gain nor this algebra proves a
pure third-order interaction; pair-specific structure can supply the gain,
and true single effects can leak outside an estimated noisy span.

SCOPE
This is descriptive support for the declared narrowed three-factor question
in a distinct same-species experiment with investigator overlap. It is not
an exact replication of the earlier four-factor selected-donor result, an
independent-laboratory replication, or a crop transfer demonstration. The
source salt exposure precedes the acute EL/HS challenge; no simultaneous-
onset, recovery, or memory interpretation follows. Published qualitative
source information and author-wide preprocessing preclude a claim of full
blindness or independently normalized held-out targets.
