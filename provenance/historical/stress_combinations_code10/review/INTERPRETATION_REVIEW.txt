CODE10 INDEPENDENT SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION REVIEW
2026-10-28 UTC

Scope: Read the frozen CODE10 protocol and completed CSV/JSON outputs; counted
signs and distinct fit IDs directly from CSV. No scientific implementation was
imported and no real gene-level calculation was rerun. Numerical files remain
unchanged. This review concerns interpretation, not a replacement for the
separate numerical audit.

MAIN INTERPRETATION

The aggregate error advantage of the pair-centroid rule recurs in both citrus
genotypes and survives all sampled complementary-pool refits. The identity and
ranking of the genes contributing to that advantage agree much better within
each genotype than between genotypes. Thus recurrence of an aggregate prediction
advantage does not imply portability of the gene-level gain profile.

Here "recurs in both genotypes" means applying the same rule separately to each
genotype's own lower-order observations. No model fitted solely in one genotype
was transferred to predict the other. Avoid unqualified "prediction transport"
or "cross-genotype transfer" if readers might understand either as that stronger
experiment. "A recurring aggregate advantage with limited between-genotype gain
profile concordance" is precise.

EVIDENCE AND EXACT DENOMINATORS

1. Aggregate score robustness under the sampled refits

There are 1,024 side-specific fits: 256 partitions x 2 genotypes x 2 sides.
Each fit was compared with 3 baselines on the same 15,466 common features.
All 3,072 mean-gain comparisons are positive. The minimum mean gain is
0.04033443816109875 squared log2(1+FPKM) units per feature. No fit failed.
The centroid therefore has smaller mean squared error than each comparator
for every sampled side-specific fit under the specified score convention.

The two sides within a partition use disjoint pools, but the 1,024 fits are NOT
mutually disjoint or independent. Different partitions repeatedly reuse the
same observed pools. Targets are excluded from fitting, but targets and all
outcomes were already seen before this diagnostic was planned. This is a
post-result robustness result, not 1,024 new validations or new test cohorts.

2. Target-repeat contribution conditional on the original predictors

Across the 6 genotype/comparator combinations, the estimated conditional stable
moment fraction ranges from 0.9792401983 to 0.9956624528. The adjusted Pearson
coefficients barely change:

Comparator                 Raw Pearson   Adjusted Pearson
second_order_factorial      0.118982       0.120981
pair_trained_factor_OLS     0.153887       0.155260
within_V3                  0.268145       0.269966

Under the zero-mean, uncorrelated repeat-error model, the observed target-pool
variability contributes little to the centered gain variance of the mean.
Consequently this particular correction does not explain away the weak
between-genotype gain association. Say "not accounted for by the target-pool
variability estimated in this conditional diagnostic," rather than "not due
to noise" or "97.9-99.6% biological signal." Shared predictor errors, correlated
pool errors and systematic preprocessing effects can remain in the stable
component. The adjusted correlation is a plug-in ratio, not unbiased truth.

3. Agreement after refitting all lower-order predictors

For each partition, within_mean averages Car-side1 versus Car-side2 and
Cleo-side1 versus Cleo-side2. cross_mean averages the two between-genotype
comparisons matched to the same 1-pool versus 2-pool side sizes.

Comparator                 Median within_mean   Median cross_mean
                           Spearman              Spearman
second_order_factorial      0.565391              0.052179
pair_trained_factor_OLS     0.584437              0.050222
within_V3                  0.714051              0.118991

Each of the 256 matched within-minus-cross contrasts is positive for every
comparator and each of Pearson, Spearman and exact-top-10%-set Jaccard. For
Spearman, minimum contrasts are 0.251605, 0.259035 and 0.397154, respectively.
These are statements about the specified means and observed partitions; do
not silently strengthen them into every individual within-pair correlation
exceeding every individual cross-pair correlation.

The median of the matched difference is not generally equal to the difference
of the two marginal medians. Report the recorded difference summary if using
that statistic. Quantile ranges across partitions are sensitivity ranges,
not confidence intervals. All-positive signs supply no calibrated P value.

WHAT THIS ADDS BEYOND THE TARGET-REPEAT DIAGNOSTIC

Diagnostic A conditions on a single set of fitted lower-order predictors.
Diagnostic B varies the observed pools used for both lower-order fitting and
target scoring, and compares two complementary sides within each genotype.
The within-versus-between contrast persists under these full refits. It is
therefore not an artifact solely of fixing one particular lower-order fit
while varying only its target pools. Together, A and B support an observed,
repeatable difference in comparator-relative gain profiles between the two
genotype datasets, beyond the limited target-repeat contribution estimated
by A.

That is evidence of conditional reproducibility within these data, not a
proof of a genotype-caused transcriptional program. The refits retain shared
processing, fixed panels, and the experimental context; they do not isolate
all possible sources of reproducible genotype-associated differences.
Moderate within-genotype rank concordance also matters: the profile is more
repeatable within genotype, but not perfectly stable gene by gene. The near-1
conditional stable fractions from A must not be reported as reliability of
the complete refitting procedure measured in B.

SCIENTIFIC BOUNDARIES TO KEEP VISIBLE

* These are gene-wise differences of squared prediction error, conditional on
  a specified comparator, transformation and feature universe. They are not
  gene effects, causal contributions, differential-expression statistics or
  direct measurements of regulatory mechanisms. Baseline model errors can
  influence which genes appear most favorable to the centroid.
* All fits are scored on a common 15,466-feature panel, which makes vector
  alignment consistent. It does not make their targets the same measured
  outcomes: each genotype and partition side has its own target pools.
* Fitting still uses the full original genotype-specific panels (17,470 and
  16,247 features), so common scoring does not remove differences in the
  contexts used for global OLS or span estimation. A difference in gain
  profiles need not isolate a gene-intrinsic genotype response difference.
* Feature filtering was fixed from the parent analysis and is shared between
  refit sides. No independent refiltering or fresh feature discovery occurs.
  Shared selection and author preprocessing remain conditioning information.
* There are only 3 RNA pools per condition. Pools are the repeated measurement
  units; features and overlapping partitions do not create more independent
  biological replication. One-pool and two-pool sides have different sampling
  properties, which the matched comparison addresses only in its stated way.
* Results use author FPKM and squared errors on log2(1+FPKM). Repeated-pool
  checks do not remove compositional, reference-mapping, annotation or shared
  normalization effects, nor establish invariance to expression scale.
* Two genotypes from one experimental study cannot establish general
  portability across species, stress combinations, field conditions, labs or
  crops. Cross-genotype gain associations are low, not absent; do not describe
  the contributing genes as wholly different or mutually exclusive.
* The analytical ingredients are established moment/cross-product and
  complementary-partition diagnostics. These results do not establish a new
  estimator, a novel biological mechanism or a publication-level novelty
  claim by themselves.

SUGGESTED RESULTS PARAGRAPH

On 15,466 common features, the pair-centroid rule retained lower mean squared
error than all three comparators in every sampled side-specific refit
(1,024 fits across two genotypes and 256 complementary-pool partitions).
Conditional target-repeat correction changed cross-genotype Pearson gain
correlations only slightly (0.119 to 0.121, 0.154 to 0.155, and 0.268 to 0.270).
After independently refitting complementary pool sides, median within-genotype
mean Spearman concordance was 0.565, 0.584 and 0.714, compared with matched
between-genotype means of 0.052, 0.050 and 0.119. Each matched contrast was
positive across the sampled partitions. These post-result diagnostics show
that recurring aggregate accuracy need not entail a portable gene-level gain
profile; they describe repeatability within the observed pools and shared
analysis framework, rather than new independent validation or causal genotype
effects.
