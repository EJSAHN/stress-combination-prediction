CODE10 — PRECOMPUTATION STATISTICAL DESIGN REVIEW
Scope: design review only. No CODE10 gain reliability or partition-refit results were inspected or calculated for this note. Reviewed CODE9_PROTOCOL.json, READ_ME_FIRST.txt and CODE8 fitting conventions. CODE8/CODE9 results are already known; CODE10 is a post-result explanatory sensitivity analysis.

VERDICT
Proceed with the two complementary diagnostics, conditional on the safeguards below. A estimates the part of the observed gene-gain dispersion that is reproducible across target pools while holding the fitted predictors fixed. B asks whether gene-gain patterns persist when both predictor construction and target evaluation use disjoint observed pools. Neither identifies a causal genotype effect, true biological pathways, an independent experiment, or a noise-free biological response space.

A. FROZEN-PREDICTOR MOMENT DIAGNOSTIC
Use exactly the 15,466 Feature_GIDs shared by the two frozen primary panels, in one identical deterministic order. Use the three primary comparators only. For each genotype and comparator, retain the three gain vectors x_j = (b-y_j)^2-(d-y_j)^2, j=1,2,3. Let H center a vector over these G features, and define <u,v> = (Hu)'(Hv)/G. Using G rather than G-1 is a consistent finite-feature convention, not an assumption that features are independent biological replicates.

Let xbar = mean_j x_j, Vobs=<xbar,xbar>, and
Nmean = sum_j <x_j-xbar,x_j-xbar> / [n(n-1)].
Then Vsig = Vobs-Nmean satisfies the exact identity
Vsig = sum_{j != k} <x_j,x_k> / [n(n-1)].
Proof: sum_j ||H(x_j-xbar)||^2 = sum_j ||Hx_j||^2-n||Hxbar||^2; substituting leaves [(sum_j Hx_j)'(sum_j Hx_j)-sum_j ||Hx_j||^2]/[G n(n-1)].

If, conditional on the frozen predictors, x_j=m+e_j with zero-mean errors and zero cross-pool error covariance, E(Vsig)=<m,m>. Cross-feature covariance is allowed: no independent-gene assumption is required. Pool variances may differ provided the common conditional mean and zero cross-pool covariance assumptions hold. This is a target-pool-variable component, including biological pooling variability; it must not be described as isolated technical measurement error. Systematic shared batch/preprocessing errors or nonexchangeable pool means can remain in the stable component. Lower-order fitting noise is repeated in the fixed b,d and is not removed.

For genotypes a,b, Cab=<xbar_a,xbar_b> is an unbiased conditional-mean inner product only if target errors are uncorrelated across genotypes. Report raw Pearson and Spearman, Vobs, Nmean, Vsig, and Vsig/Vobs when Vobs>0. Algebraically Nmean>=0 and Vsig<=Vobs (up to rounding); Vsig may be negative. If either Vsig is nonpositive, the corrected correlation is unavailable with an explicit reason. Otherwise rho_adj=Cab/sqrt(Vsig_a*Vsig_b) is a plug-in moment ratio, NOT an unbiased correlation estimator. It can exceed [-1,1] at n=3; retain the value and flag it rather than clipping or calling it a true correlation. Do not apply the Pearson correction to Spearman. Do not give gene-bootstrap confidence intervals, gene-level P values, or treat G=15,466 as the RNA replicate count.

Within-genotype report each of the three target-pool pairs, not merely their average. A high within-pool correlation is conditional on shared frozen predictor terms, so it does not resolve predictor-fit instability. Also check the identity x_j-xbar=-2(b-d)*(y_j-ybar). Direct squared-error, factorized-gain and off-diagonal moment calculations should agree numerically. Zero centered variance makes the associated ordinary correlation unavailable.

B. DISJOINT FULL-REFIT PARTITION SENSITIVITY
Freeze 256 complete split vectors per genotype before new numerical results. A complete vector has eight indices, one of {0,1,2} for each condition, hence 3^8=6,561 possible vectors per genotype. Sample 256 unique complete vectors uniformly without replacement; individual condition choices necessarily recur. Use independent RNG streams per genotype. Save the exact chosen sample names, condition order, complement indices, seeds and partition-file hash. Computational draw pairing across genotypes does not assert experimentally matched RNA pools.

For every condition, side 1 contains the chosen single pool and side 2 the remaining two pools. Fit each side separately using its own CT, singles and pairs; never use its triple target to fit coefficients or basis. Equal-weight means across conditions preserve the parent estimand. Retain CODE8 log2(1+FPKM), the full parent primary genotype-specific gene panels, original SVD/rank convention, and np.linalg.lstsq(..., rcond=None). Fit the OLS coefficients and single-response span on each FULL genotype-specific panel, then restrict scoring comparisons to the 15,466 common IDs. Refitting on the intersection would change the model and is not this analysis.

Refit pair centroid D and the three comparators separately for each side. Recompute CT reference, single and pair displacements, V3 basis and OLS coefficients; record numerical ranks, coefficients and failure/degeneracy flags. Do not import frozen whole-dataset coefficients. Side-2 gain may be calculated using its mean target: the difference of the two squared errors against that mean equals the average per-pool squared-error difference because target-variance terms cancel exactly. This equality does not apply to an absolute MSE, which must retain its stated target convention.

Compute Pearson, Spearman and top-k Jaccard on the same common universe for six pairings: Car1/Car2, Cleo1/Cleo2, Car1/Cleo2, Car2/Cleo1, Car1/Cleo1 and Car2/Cleo2. The first four give the primary sample-size-matched one-pool/two-pool comparison; cross 1/1 and 2/2 are secondary. For each draw and each metric, also record matched contrast = mean(Car1/Car2, Cleo1/Cleo2) - mean(Car1/Cleo2, Car2/Cleo1). This is a descriptive within-minus-cross contrast with matched one-pool/two-pool sample sizes, no numerical success threshold and no P value. Summarize its quantiles as partition sensitivity, not confidence limits. Unequal biological variance or model identifiability between genotypes still prevents a causal reading of within/cross differences. Top k means exactly ceil(0.10*15,466)=1,547 descending gains, lexical Feature_GID tie-break, without conditioning membership on gain positivity. Record empty/constant vector conditions; do not replace unavailable correlations with zero.

These are overlapping partitions of the same 48 pools, not 256 independent experiments. Report quantiles as partition-sensitivity summaries, never confidence intervals, sampling P values or discovery replication. Use all preselected partitions; no outcome-dependent seed, number of draws, top fraction or comparator tuning. Partition uniqueness is per full vector, not an impossible requirement of 256 unique choices per condition. The original full-data panel filter used both sides' CT/single pools, so the sides are sample-disjoint conditional on a shared selected feature panel; this is not fully independent validation. Any source-level preprocessing shared across libraries likewise remains shared.

INTERPRETATION AND COMPUTATION
If A is stable but B is not, fixed-predictor target repeatability did not survive refitting. If B within-genotype agreement is consistently above size-matched between-genotype agreement, say that attribution differences persist under these observed-pool partitions; do not claim a causal genotype mechanism or complete removal of noise. If both are unstable, gene-level attribution is weak even though the frozen aggregate CODE8 prediction result may remain. No new numerical go/no-go threshold should replace the parent primary criterion.

The 256 draws imply 1,024 small full-panel fits (2 genotypes x 2 sides x 256), each with three-column linear algebra; this is a reasonable local workload. Use one BLAS thread for reproducibility and avoid saving large per-draw gene matrices. Preserve partition draws, coefficients/ranks, per-draw association/set summaries, runtime, exact code/protocol/source hashes and all failure flags. Keep CODE8/CODE9 files unchanged. Report all three comparators even if their stories differ. Original parent outcomes remain known throughout; this study has not gained a new independent validation dataset.
