CODE7 simulation: independent numerical and interpretation review
28 October 2026

PASS: independent full-gene-vector SVD replay reproduces 216 selected scenario
outcomes (six draw IDs across all 36 cells), including the empirical-only
compact-coordinate branch and both full-gene covariance mixtures. All five
paired scores agree; largest score difference is 3.64e-15. All 180 summary
rows were independently recomputed from all 36,000 stored draws. Source hashes,
fixed feature panels, maps, protocol pinning and the fixed reporting flag agree.

Joint latent means were independently reconstructed from their row-Gram
eigensystem; maximum difference from direct SVD is 1.69e-13. Both nulls have
three spanning latent single directions. All zero-noise gains are numerical
zero. Independently computed covariance traces and all 108 analytic oracle
expectations agree. A separate small synthetic covariance/Monte Carlo check
also passed and is reported in ORACLE_EXPECTATION_CHECK.json.

WT observed T is 0.062273019. The joint-rank-three, empirical-covariance,
unit-SD model has q95=0.064190945 and 56/1000 draws at least as large as T.
The fixed flag that T exceed all six unit-noise q95 values therefore fails.
This is a borderline conditional range comparison with Monte Carlo SE about
0.00727 for the fraction. It is not a calibrated biological P-value or evidence
that noise caused the signal. The imposed latent orientation and covariance
are substantially uncertain with three RNA pools per condition.

A model reproducing a scalar statistic need not reproduce the full expression
dataset or its mean-residual directions. In particular, rank-two empirical
covariance has restricted support. Mean-fit MSE divided by estimated noise
trace is only a scale diagnostic; it is not a goodness-of-fit test.

The root READ_ME_FIRST.txt was reviewed. Its mixed interpretation is faithful:
CODE6's prediction performance remains, and much of the outside contribution
survives the observed replicate-cloud expansion, while the stronger biological
claim about an unknown true response dimension remains unresolved. No primary
endpoint is rescued by choosing another covariance mixture, genotype or scale.
The T simulations do not calibrate the distinct disjoint-reference margins.

DIAGNOSTIC FLOATING-POINT NOTE
Strict '<0' latent-log and negative-probability diagnostics are sensitive to
roundoff for genes with zero variance and means at numerical zero. Equivalent
floating-point paths can shift their reported fractions by up to 0.002816.
All strict diagnostics lie within the independently calculated +/-1e-12
mean-boundary envelopes. Supplementary rates below -1e-12 are recorded in
NEGATIVE_SCALE_TOLERANCE_DIAGNOSTIC.csv. This reviewer-only diagnostic does
not alter any production latent means, noise, simulated scores or summaries.
Negative working-scale diagnostics are not biological validity tests.

Zero of 1000 exceedances likewise does not establish zero tail probability;
the plug-in Monte Carlo SE is zero at the count boundary. The supplied count
and finite Monte Carlo resolution must remain visible.
