GEO inputs: GSE147962, GSE330879 and GSE203331. The exact four processed files,
accession pages, source URLs, units, sizes and hashes are in config/data_sources.json.
The local reproduction distribution retains these necessary inputs. The prepared
GitHub distribution excludes them and provides scripts/fetch_inputs.py.

NCBI's Molecular Data Usage policy states that NCBI places no restrictions on
use or distribution of molecular data, including gene expression. NCBI also
does not transfer or certify rights held by original contributors. This package
does not describe third-party data as newly licensed by its code authors.
Official sources checked 28 October 2026:
https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/home/about/policies/
https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/disclaimer.html

Frozen derived predictor arrays and reference tables retain the original data
identifiers and accession attribution. Their inclusion enables reproducibility
checks; they are not newly collected measurements.

Citrus functional annotations were frozen from NCBI exact-Entrez GO associations
and a compatible Gene Ontology snapshot. Their provenance and mapping limits
are preserved with the annotation assets. Cite the Gene Ontology resource and
the corresponding NCBI annotation release when reporting those analyses.
Do not describe these computational annotations as experimentally validated.

Python, NumPy, pandas, SciPy, matplotlib, Pillow and openpyxl are external
dependencies and retain their own licenses. They are not bundled or installed
by the local launcher.
