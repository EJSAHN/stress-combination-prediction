Figure 1. Lower-order observations predict the combined three-stress response.
(A) Full three-factor designs in Arabidopsis thaliana (GSE330879: salt, excess light, heat) and citrus (GSE203331: water stress, high light, heat). Filled cells denote applied factors; the triple condition is orange. Each genotype has three RNA pools in each of eight conditions. The pair-centroid prediction averages the three pair-condition means on the log2(1 + author-deposited FPKM) scale. Predictor construction excludes numerical triple-condition values. Each genotype uses its own lower-order observations. (B) Mean squared error (MSE), averaged over the three target pools and retained features, divided by that genotype's control-prediction MSE. The single-span model retains the pair centroid's projection into the span of the three observed single-response means. (C) Additional citrus baselines: mean singles, a scalar fitted to pair responses, three factor-specific weights fitted to pair responses, and the second-order factorial model. The dotted line marks control MSE. Primary filtering retains 19,180 Col-0, 19,034 rap2.3, 17,470 Carrizo and 16,247 Cleopatra features. Ratios compare prediction performance within each application, not stress intensity across studies. These public studies have overlapping investigators and are not independent laboratories.

Figure 2. Gains outside an observed response span do not establish a new biological dimension.
(A, B) MSE gain from the pair-centroid component outside successively expanded observed spans in Arabidopsis and citrus. Spans use three single-condition means, all nine single-condition pools, and those pools plus control-pool variation; ranks are 3, 9 and 11. Points average the same three target pools. Percentages are final outside-span gains divided by the mean-only outside-span gain. (C, D) Conditional simulated distributions for Col-0 and rap2.3 under a joint rank-three latent-mean construction, empirical residual covariance and unit noise standard-deviation multiplier. Each histogram contains 1,000 draws. Red dashed lines show observed gains; black dotted lines show conditional 95th percentiles. Counts identify draws at least as large as observed, not biological P-values. Latent means use already observed noncontrol conditions, including the triple; this uncertainty construction is not a predictor. Three RNA pools cannot identify the true high-dimensional covariance or span. Matching this statistic does not establish that noise explains the full expression dataset. All scenarios appear in Figure S3.

Figure 3. The distribution and concentration of citrus prediction gains depend on the comparator.
Gain is comparator squared error minus pair-centroid squared error, averaged over three target pools. (A, B) Original net gain retained after excluding the highest mean-gain features from scoring in Carrizo and Cleopatra. Exactly the ceiling of 1%, 5% or 10% of the panel is removed, with lexical feature-ID ties. Predictions are unchanged; the dashed line marks zero net gain. This outcome-dependent score deletion is not experimental gene removal or biological sparsity. (C) Fraction with positive mean gain in the original primary panels (17,470 Carrizo; 16,247 Cleopatra). Features are not independent biological replicates. (D) Original comparator MSE and its minimum after target-fitted unconstrained global rescaling of the displacement from control. Values are divided by original pair-centroid MSE, shown by the dashed line at one. This rescaling is a post-result diagnostic, not a predictive competitor.

Figure 4. Gene-gain profiles agree more strongly within genotype than between genotypes.
All panels align 15,466 exact feature IDs shared by the primary citrus panels. (A) Estimated repeat-stable centered gain moment divided by the observed moment of the mean, conditional on fixed predictions and three target pools per genotype. Shared fitting and preprocessing effects remain; this is not heritability or a fraction of biological signal. (B) Observed cross-genotype Pearson association and its target-repeat moment correction. The corrected value is a nonlinear plug-in ratio, not unbiased truth; Spearman is not corrected. (C, D) Spearman rank concordance and top-10%-set Jaccard overlap after complementary-pool refitting. In each of 256 fixed partitions per genotype, one pool per condition forms one side and the other two form the other side. Lower-order fits use each side's full genotype panel before common-feature scoring. Within values average Carrizo-side1/side2 and Cleopatra-side1/side2; between values average the two size-matched cross-genotype orientations. Points are medians; bars span the 2.5th–97.5th partition percentiles, not confidence intervals. Top sets contain exactly 1,547 features. Partitions reuse the same pools and retain shared filtering and preprocessing. No fitted coefficients are transferred between genotypes. These post-result diagnostics are not new independent validations or evidence of causal genotype programs.

Figure S1. Exploratory discovery and the limits of selecting quiet donors.
(A) Four-factor prediction in the discovery Arabidopsis dataset GSE147962, using 21,495 features and three target pools. Values are log10(MSE/control MSE): zero denotes equal error and negative values favor the model. Selected-triple and selected-residual predictions use PQ+HL+HS and S+PQ+HL donors selected during discovery. Selection used prior outcomes; this is not an independent test of a universal donor rule. (B) Rank-two donor-selection comparisons across four triple targets and the four-factor target. Triple targets use their eligible pair donors; the four-factor target uses triple donors. Colors and labels show log10(MSE/control MSE), with full precision in source data. Low-common, low-raw and low-residual rules average the two lowest-scoring eligible donors by mean squared control-centered common, raw or residual displacement, respectively. The last column uses only the low-common pair's residual component. Low-common and low-raw choices coincide; no selector consistently outperforms the alternatives. S, salt; PQ, paraquat; HL, high light; HS, heat stress. Discovery expression is log2(1 + author-normalized count), not FPKM.

Figure S2. Target-pool and expression-filter sensitivity.
(A–D) Individual target-pool MSE divided by the same pool's control-prediction MSE for Col-0, rap2.3, Carrizo and Cleopatra. The three horizontally offset points per model represent three RNA pools, not gene-level replicates or confidence intervals. Dotted lines indicate equal MSE to control. (E) Pair-centroid MSE relative to control after lower-order mean-FPKM thresholds of 0.5, 1 and 2. Each mean uses three target pools; thresholds are sensitivity analyses, not independent cohorts. (F) Outside-span gain remaining after expansion from single-condition means to all single and control pools, divided by the mean-only outside-span gain. The primary threshold is 1 FPKM.

Figure S3. All conditional noise scenarios.
(A–D) Col-0 and rap2.3 under observed single-mean-span and joint rank-three latent-mean constructions. The 36 genotype-by-latent-model-by-covariance-by-scale scenarios each contain 1,000 draws. Colors identify diagonal covariance fractions: zero retains empirical within-condition residual covariance, one retains its diagonal, and 0.5 mixes the two. Points show medians and bars the 2.5th–97.5th conditional percentiles. Dashed lines show observed gains. These are simulation ranges, not confidence intervals. Errors are reused across appropriate settings; 36,000 outcomes are not independent biological observations. Mean and covariance assumptions remain conditional on three pools per condition.

Figure S4. Computational functional annotations provide descriptive context.
(A, B) Mean gene gain relative to the second-order model in Carrizo and Cleopatra. (C, D) Gain relative to fitted single-response weights. Gene Ontology groups were fixed before gene-gain calculation and propagated through is_a and part_of relations. Exact Entrez mappings use NCBI annotation release 103 and the matching 2023-07-27 ontology; all associations have computational IEA evidence. Features with duplicated Entrez assignments are excluded. Numbers label features, not biological replicates. Open markers denote fewer than 20 features. Dashed lines mark zero gain; dotted lines mark the eligible BP-annotated background mean. Groups overlap and are not exhaustive. Any-GO coverage uses the original primary panel as denominator. These are prediction-error summaries, not pathway activation or enrichment tests. Gain estimates average three target RNA pools per genotype.
